Why this page?

Advocat is built and operated by Vorantis OÜ, an Estonian company. Our AI legal assistant is currently offered to consumers only in Finland and Estonia, where the underlying legal framework permits AI-assisted self-help for individuals. In your country, the rules around providing legal information differ, and we serve only licensed law firms and corporate legal teams there.

For law firms and corporate customers

If you operate a law firm or in-house legal function, our B2B offering is available: per-seat licensing, German/French/local-law DPA available, EU-hosted infrastructure, and integration support for your existing tools. Contact sales@advocat.ee or use the form below.
