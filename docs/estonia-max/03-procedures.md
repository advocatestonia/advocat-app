**Sources:** eesti.ee, politsei.ee, emta.ee, rik.ee,
sotsiaalkindlustusamet.ee, tootukassa.ee, aki.ee, ti.ee.
**Fetched:** 2026-04-21.

## eesti.ee — state portal

- **URL:** https://www.eesti.ee/en
- **Languages:** Estonian, English, Russian
- **User groups:** Citizens, Entrepreneurs, Officials
- **8 main categories:** Citizenship & documents, Taxes, Family,
  Work & employment, Transport, Health, Housing, Business

## Major agency service entry points

### PPA (Police and Border Guard Board) — immigration hub
- **Portal:** https://www.politsei.ee/en/instructions
- **e-Appointment:** https://broneering.politsei.ee
- **e-Applications:** https://etaotlus.politsei.ee
- **D-visa:** long-stay up to 365 days, file 30 days before current
  stay expires, not earlier than 2 months
- **Residence permit (employment):** up to 5 years, extendable 10 years
- **Residence permit (family):** up to 5 years, max inviter's permit
- **Long-term resident's permit:** requires 5 years temporary residence

### MTA (Tax and Customs Board)
- **Portal:** https://www.emta.ee/en
- **e-MTA:** https://maasikas.emta.ee
- **Forms:** TSD (payroll, 10th of following month), KMD (VAT, 20th
  of following month), personal income declaration (30 April deadline)

### RIK (Centre of Registers and Information Systems) — business
- **Portal:** https://www.rik.ee/en
- **e-Business Register:** https://ariregister.rik.ee/
- **OÜ registration fee:** €265 (digital, minutes if all founders
  have Estonian e-authentication)
- **Min share capital:** €0 (since 2023)
- **Annual report deadline:** 6 months after year end

### Sotsiaalkindlustusamet (Social Insurance Board)
- **Portal:** https://sotsiaalkindlustusamet.ee/en
- **Self-service:** https://iseteenindus.sotsiaalkindlustusamet.ee/
- **Services:** pensions, family benefits, disability, victim support,
  elatisabi (child maintenance support), foreign pensions

### Töötukassa (Unemployment Fund)
- **Portal:** https://www.tootukassa.ee/en
- **Eligibility:** 180 days worked in 12 months prior to registration
- **Benefit duration:** 180-300 days
- **Limitation period (wage claims):** 3 years

### Tööinspektsioon (Labour Inspectorate)
- **Portal:** https://www.ti.ee/en
- **Advisory:** +372 640 6000, Mon-Fri 09:00-15:00, free
- **Labour dispute committee decisions:** within 45 days
- **Language:** dispute proceedings in Estonian only; translations
  required for foreign-language evidence

### AKI (Data Protection Inspectorate)
- **Portal:** https://www.aki.ee/en
- **Advisory line:** +372 5620 2341, Mon-Thu 13:00-16:00
- **Email:** info@aki.ee
- **Address:** Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn
