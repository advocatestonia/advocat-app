## Victim support (Ohvriabi) — critical for Sulga-class cases

- **Helpline:** 116 006 (or +372 614 7393 from abroad)
- **Web chat:** https://www.palunabi.ee
- **24/7, anonymous, free, in ET/EN/RU**
- **Services:**
  - Domestic violence victims
  - Sexual violence victims
  - Human trafficking victims
  - Hate crime victims
  - All crime victims (support through criminal proceedings)
  - Psychological first aid
  - Referral to shelters, legal aid, state compensation

## State legal aid (Riigi õigusabi)

- **Portal:** https://www.advokatuur.ee/en/state-legal-aid
- **Eligibility:** natural person residing in Estonia or another EU
  Member State; or citizen of Estonia/EU
- **Cost structures:** free / partial reimbursement / full reimbursement
  (state pre-pays, recovers later based on income)
- **Application:** to maakohus of applicant's residence; financial
  situation notice required except for criminal defence
- **Alternative:** HUGO.legal / juristaitab.ee — 2h free legal
  consultation for gross income ≤ €1,200/month, in ET/EN/RU

## Integration Foundation (INSA / Integratsiooni Sihtasutus)

- **Portal:** https://www.integratsioon.ee/ (ET/EN/RU)
- **Free Estonian language courses** A1, A2, B1 for newly arrived
  immigrants (< 5 years in Estonia)
- **Settle in Estonia programme:** adaptation modules — basic, work,
  study, family, research, international protection
- **Free citizenship preparation:** 160 hours per language level
- **Info line:** 800 9999 (EE) / +372 6599 025 (abroad)
- **Email:** info@integratsiooniinfo.ee

## Ombudsman (Õiguskantsler)

- **URL:** https://www.oiguskantsler.ee/
- **Phone:** +372 693 8404
- **Address:** Kohtu 8, 15193 Tallinn
- **Competence:**
  - Constitutional review of laws
  - Review of public authority actions
  - Human rights ombudsman
  - Children's rights ombudsman
  - National Preventive Mechanism (torture prevention)
- **Complaint deadline:** 1 year (recommended) from awareness of violation

## AI safety rule

If the user shows signs of crisis (mentions suicide, domestic violence,
trafficking, being in danger), the AI MUST **immediately** surface the
relevant helpline number — 112 for immediate danger, 116 006 for crime
victims, 116 111 for children, 1247 for general crisis. Do not make the
user ask.
