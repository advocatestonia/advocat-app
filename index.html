    Migri refused to extend my residence permit. The letter mentions 30 days. What do I do?

    IM
    Immigration lawyer

    This decision is appealable. You have 30 days from notification to appeal to the Administrative Court — Aliens Act (ulkomaalaislaki) § 190.

    AA
    Senior asianajaja (FI)

    Agreed on the appeal. One more thing: keep your job — while the appeal is pending, your right to work usually continues.

    LT
    Litigator (KHO + Riigikohus)

    I push back: an appeal alone is slow — it takes months. File a new application on updated grounds in parallel. Two tracks, not one.

    Positions diverge — the chairman must address both.
    Chairman · synthesis

    Unanimous: appeal within 30 days. On strategy the majority favours a parallel new application. The appeal is drafted — waiting for your approval.
