Data Privacy Notice

Please review before using the call assistant

Your call audio is processed by Deepgram (US) for transcription and AI suggestions are provided by Anthropic (US).

Audio is NOT stored permanently — it is streamed in real-time and discarded after processing.

Transcripts are kept only in your browser and are never sent to any server except for AI analysis (which uses only the text, not the audio).

When enabled, all transcript data is automatically deleted when you close or leave this page. Nothing persists in localStorage.
